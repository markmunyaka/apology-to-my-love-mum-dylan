❤️
❤️Mary

My dearest Mary ❤️,

I'm sitting here with a heavy heart, and I can't stop thinking about how much I hurt you. It's honestly breaking me inside knowing that I'm the reason behind your pain 😔 If I could turn back time, I would undo everything just to see you smile again.

You didn't deserve any of that… not my words, not my actions, not the way I made you feel. You deserve softness, patience, and a love that feels safe every single day 💔 And instead, I caused you distress and that's something I will regret deeply.

Mary, you are so important to me. You're not just someone I love… you're a part of me. My heart feels tied to yours in a way I can't even explain 💖 The thought of losing you or being the reason you pull away scares me more than anything. It makes me realize just how much you truly mean to me.

I keep replaying everything in my mind, wishing I had chosen better words, wishing I had held you with more care, wishing I had protected your heart the way I should have 😢 I hate that I failed you in that moment.

Please believe me when I say this my love for you is real, deep, and unshaken ❤️✨ Even now, especially now, I feel it stronger because I realize how much I can't afford to lose you. You are my peace, my happiness, my safe place… and I hate that I became the source of your pain.

I want to be better for you, Mary. I want to grow, to learn, to love you in a way that heals, not hurts 💕 I want to be the one who lifts you up, who listens, who understands you without making you feel small or unimportant.

I miss you so much… I miss your presence, your voice, your warmth 😞 It feels empty knowing things aren't okay between us, especially because it's my fault.

I'm not perfect, but my love for you is true. And I'm willing to do whatever it takes to make things right, to rebuild your trust, and to show you every single day how much you mean to me 💞

Please forgive me, my love. I need you… and I love you more than I can ever put into words ❤️

Forever and always yours ❤️

Things I love about you

  • Your beautiful smile that lights up my world
  • Your kindness that touches everyone around you
  • Your patience and understanding
  • The way you make every day better just by being in it
